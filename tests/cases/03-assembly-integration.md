# 03 · Larger assembly integration

**Fixture**

Use a disposable copy of a robot or machine assembly with at least ten existing occurrences and two levels of hierarchy. Set up a mounting region at least 200 × 80 × 75 mm with four mounting points on a 160 × 60 mm rectangular pattern. Keep an identifiable adjacent obstruction near the travel region. Record the baseline occurrence paths, pre-existing joint names and active version, and supply a copy of case 02's slide or identify it as a prerequisite.

**Prompt**

> Integrate the supplied 60 mm rack slide into the identified mounting region on a copy of my larger Fusion assembly. Use the existing four-point interface. Preserve unrelated component placements, existing joints, and external references. Check 2 mm clearance to the identified obstruction at 0, 30 and 60 mm and any closer pose apparent from the geometry. Report any conflict before changing other subsystems. Provide the editable assembly and a concise verification report.

**Pass criteria**

- Baseline records the correct document and target occurrence. A version or copy is identified. Before-and-after evidence shows unrelated occurrences, transforms, linked assets and joints are preserved.
- The slide is identifiable in the hierarchy, mounted correctly and still moves through its intended stroke. Report measured clearances and interference results with checked poses and occurrence names.
- A supplied fixture with an intentionally introduced obstacle inside the sweep produces a reported conflict. A fixture with positive clearance below 2 mm produces a reported clearance failure even if interference reports zero volume. Use copies of the fixture for these reruns.
- Existing content remains editable where it was editable at baseline. Unsupported or unsampled checks are explicitly recorded. If the fixture happens to be competition-bound, check season rules only when the prompt calls for them.
