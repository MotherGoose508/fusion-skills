# 01 · Parametric motor mount

**Prompt**

> Build an editable Autodesk Fusion motor mounting plate with a 100 × 80 × 6 mm rectangular 6061 aluminium blank, machined from flat stock with a mill and drilled through from one side. Centre a Ø25 mm shaft clearance hole and four Ø4.5 mm motor holes on a 32 × 32 mm square pattern. Add four Ø5.5 mm frame holes at x = ±40 mm and y = ±30 mm from the plate centre. I need to adjust the plate thickness and motor hole pitch later. Provide the native model and a brief verification report.

**Pass criteria**

- Native design measures 100 × 80 × 6 mm and all nine through features match the prompt, within 0.1 mm in measured nominal CAD geometry.
- Named thickness and motor pitch parameters drive geometry. Change thickness to 8 mm and both motor pitches to 36 mm, or their shared parameter, then verify updated values, stationary frame holes, and no relevant timeline warnings. Restore and recheck nominal dimensions.
- Feature order and names make the motor and frame interfaces easy to find. Report minimum hole-to-edge distance and any motor-to-frame fastener or shaft access risks. Missing hardware dimensions remain assumptions.
- Report actual inspection evidence, the nominal state, and physical fit items. Capture the parameter table and a dimensioned inspection view if available.
